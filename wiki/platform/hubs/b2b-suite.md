# b2b suite

This hub covers the Shopware B2B Suite (`SwagB2bPlatform`): its concepts, Core and Storefront guides, installation, and merchant-side usage. Come here for B2B Suite questions such as ACL, audit log, CRUD/listing patterns, ajax panels or overriding services, instead of grepping. The 6.6 and 6.7 developer trees document the same guides; the 6.7 pages are near-duplicates of the 6.6 ones with updated details (for example `services.php` instead of `services.xml`). The B2B Suite is unsupported from Shopware 6.8; the 6.7 B2B Suite Migration pages describe moving data to B2B Components.
