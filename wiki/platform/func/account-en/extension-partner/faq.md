## What it is
An FAQ page for Shopware Extension Partners answering common questions about the extension partner program.

## Key steps / config
- Changing your extension prefix: if you have not yet created an extension, email `alliances@shopware.com` to request a custom prefix; if you already offer store extensions, contact support by phone or email to check options.
- Support ticket escalation: an email reminder is sent when tickets are open; if unanswered, a second reminder with roughly a 3 business day deadline follows; if the deadline passes without processing, Shopware calls to discuss; as a last step, if the call is unsuccessful, apps are deactivated in the store and commission payments blocked until contact is made.
- Using your own extensions in your own shop: possible if you are listed as the Extension Partner in the imprint of the affected store and the apps are offered as rental versions in the store; email the affected apps to `alliances@shopware.com`.
- Requesting a preview: in the Extension Partner area, request a preview from within the extension itself (maintain description/translation first); a confirmed preview reserves the function in the store.
- Uploading an extension: review the Quality Guide before uploading.
- Creating a test environment: in the vendor area under Wildcard Environments, create a wildcard environment by selecting a domain; a subdomain creates an instance, and multiple instances can exist within one environment.
- App download numbers: updated daily for every extension with at least one download.

## Essential identifiers
- `alliances@shopware.com`
