## What it is

A short pointer page for merchants on Shopware Professional who are considering a switch to a Shopware plan (e.g. Evolve).

## When to use

When evaluating what moving from Shopware Professional to a Shopware plan means: edition differences, scope of services, general conditions, process, technical requirements, and organisational aspects.

## Key steps / config

- Shopware publishes an information page comparing the editions' scope of services and general conditions when switching from Professional.
- A migration FAQ section answers typical questions about the switch, its process, technical requirements, and organisational aspects.
- Partners who need a wildcard environment for their customers should consult the partner section of the documentation.

## Essential identifiers

- `wildcard environment` (partner-section documentation topic)
