## What it is

An overview of the different places a Shopware merchant can get help, and how the available channels depend on which plan is booked.

## When to use

When deciding who to contact for a question or problem: official Shopware support, the community forum, a certified partner agency, or a self-service learning path.

## Key steps / config

- **Plan differences**: the Community Edition is free and includes all basic functions but no official support; the chargeable plans (**Shopware Rise**, **Shopware Evolve**, **Shopware Beyond**) add advanced features and official support, priced by shop size.
- **Shopware support**: the first contact point for anyone with a paid plan. It helps with shop setup and configuration of frontend and backend, with reaction times defined by the booked subscription. Larger subscriptions add access to **developer support** (answers implementation questions, including about installed plugins, but does not build individual solutions) and some subscriptions add **emergency support** for serious issues outside normal working hours.
- **Community forum**: open to all user types, including Community Edition shop owners. Registration is anonymous and unrelated to the Shopware account. It works on a "shop owners help shop owners" basis, so users are asked to share solutions they find themselves.
- **Shopware partners**: certified agencies that have passed a Shopware quality/service check. They can implement individual functions, set up a shop entirely, or answer questions the community could not — as a paid service.
- **Shopware Community Hub**: a central space with interactive learning paths to explore Shopware step by step and connect with other users.

## Essential identifiers

- Editions/plans: `Community Edition`, `Shopware Rise`, `Shopware Evolve`, `Shopware Beyond`
- Support channels: Shopware support, developer support, emergency support, community forum, Shopware partners, Shopware Community Hub

## Gotchas

- Official Shopware support (including developer and emergency support) is only available with a paid plan, not the free Community Edition.
- The community forum is unrelated to a merchant's Shopware account, so no shop information is tied to a forum post by default.
