## What it is
This page gives a first overview of the structure and functions of the Shopware 6 Administration.

## When to use
Use it to orient yourself in the Administration interface after first login.

## Key steps / config
- Access: append `/admin` to the shop domain (e.g. `www.myshop.com/admin`) to reach the login page, then sign in with your username and password.
- General: the Administration is a single central location; it can be opened in parallel in several browser tabs.
- Menu bar (left side), divided into:
  - Shopware version indicator, at the top.
  - Configuration areas — view orders, manage customers, create Shopping Experiences.
  - Sales channel section — existing channels are listed; add more via the plus symbol.
  - Minimize menu — collapses the menu to icon-only view for more editing space.
  - Profile settings, at the bottom left — change the display language and log out.
- Search: a central search box at the top searches products, categories, customers, orders and media from any menu.
- Notifications: the bell icon at the top right shows system or plugin update notifications.

## Essential identifiers
- `/admin`
