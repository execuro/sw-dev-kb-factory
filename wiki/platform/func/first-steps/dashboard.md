## What it is
The dashboard is the initial page shown after logging into the Administration, offering a first overview of current topics.

## When to use
Use it to see current Shopware topics, reach the Shopware Help Center, give feedback, and check order/sales statistics.

## Key steps / config
- A top link takes you directly to the Shopware Help Center.
- The "Give feedback" button takes you directly to the Issuetracker.
- Two statistics widgets show how the number of orders and sales have developed; a drop-down at the top right sets the time frame.
- The "Since yesterday" option describes all orders since midnight.

## Essential identifiers
- `Issuetracker`

## Version notes
This dashboard layout applies to the range 6.0.0 - 6.4.7.0.
