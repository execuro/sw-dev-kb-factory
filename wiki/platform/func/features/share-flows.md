## What it is

Share Flows lets merchants import flows into their system or export them, taking flows from Flow Builder and moving them between different systems, such as sending a flow to an app or shifting it from a test system to a live system.

## When to use

When migrating or distributing automated business process flows between environments, e.g. test to production, or sharing a flow via an app.

## Key steps / config

- Use Flow Builder to define and perform business processes without writing code.
- Export a flow to move it to another system, or import a flow received from elsewhere.
- Depending on the event, flows can automate sending emails, making URL calls, or changing the order status.
