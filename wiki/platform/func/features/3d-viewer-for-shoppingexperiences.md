## What it is

The 3D Viewer block for Shopping Experiences lets merchants integrate interactive 3D objects into landing, product or category pages outside of the product detail page, using the visual page builder for Shopping Experiences. Store visitors can call up the 3D elements via QR code and view them in their environment through augmented reality.

## When to use

Use it to add 3D product visualizations to any Shopping Experiences page, not just the standard product detail page.
