## What it is

The Custom Products extension lets customers design their own products, offering customizable products such as inscribed mugs or shirts with a choice of text, lettering or color.

## Key steps / config

- Tailor the selection interface to the product and options at hand.
- Interface elements range from checkboxes and text fields through to a comprehensive step-by-step mode.

- `Custom Products`
