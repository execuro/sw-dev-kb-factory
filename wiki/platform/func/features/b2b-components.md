## What it is

B2B Components add business-to-business functionality to a Shopware shop: enhanced roles and contact management (sub-accounts for a customer's employees), order approval workflows, quote management, quick ordering, and reusable order lists. These functions are available to shop customers who have been authorised accordingly by the shop operator.

## When to use

When B2B customers need to represent their own company structure in the shop — multiple employee accounts with different permissions, controlled purchasing approval, and faster reordering of frequently bought items.

## Key steps / config

- **Employee Management** — enhanced rights and permissions management lets the shop operator assign and manage rights and roles within the B2B functions.
- **Order Approvals** — the admin specifies which users should be able to approve or reject orders in the storefront; B2B customers can define their own approval rules for roles.
- **Quote Management** — merchants create quotes and send them to customers; customers can accept or reject quotes sent to them.
- **Quick Orders** — customers add items to the shopping basket or an order list quickly by entering the product number and quantity, either via a simple input mask or by importing a file.
- **Shopping list (Order list)** — customers plan and manage recurring large order quantities via saved order lists.
