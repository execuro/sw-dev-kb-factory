## What it is
Immersive Elements is an extension that adds 3D elements under the "Commerce" section of the storefront.

## When to use
Use it to turn an online store into a more immersive brand experience, as a cost-effective alternative to using external 3D resources, with the aim of increasing conversions.

## Essential identifiers
- `Immersive Elements`

## Version notes
The extension is included in the Rise, Evolve and Beyond plans.
