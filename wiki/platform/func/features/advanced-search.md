## What it is

Advanced Search extends the Shopware 6 storefront search with a high-performance search feature based on Elasticsearch, aiming to deliver fast search results and an improved user experience even with large data volumes.

## When to use

Use it when the default storefront search needs to scale to large product catalogs while keeping search results fast.

## Essential identifiers

- Underlying engine: Elasticsearch
