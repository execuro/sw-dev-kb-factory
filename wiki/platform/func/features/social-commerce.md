## What it is
Social Commerce lets merchants use social media platforms — such as Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest — as additional sales channels. In most cases this is done through an export feed that is integrated into the respective platform.

## When to use
Use it to boost brand awareness and sales by targeting your intended audience on the social media platforms they already use.

## Essential identifiers
- `Social Commerce`
- export feed
