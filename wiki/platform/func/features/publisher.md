## What it is

The Publisher lets merchants create their own designs for shop pages using Shopping Experiences: create drafts of shop pages, save them, and publish later or at a set point in time.

## Key steps / config

- Create drafts of shop pages within Shopping Experiences.
- Save drafts and publish them later or at a scheduled point in time.
- The activity feed shows who made what changes.

## Essential identifiers

- `Publisher`
- Shopping Experiences
- activity feed
