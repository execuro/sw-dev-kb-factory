## What it is

Subscriptions is a Shopware feature that lets customers order products automatically at regular intervals. Under a subscription, products are delivered at regular intervals without the customer having to actively place an order in the shop each time. The merchant defines the subscription terms. Configuration is described on the Subscriptions settings page (Settings > Shop > Subscriptions) of the merchant documentation.

## When to use

- You want to sell products via subscriptions, for example to strengthen customer loyalty.
- Customers should receive recurring deliveries without placing a new order each time.
- You need your own subscription terms for these products.
