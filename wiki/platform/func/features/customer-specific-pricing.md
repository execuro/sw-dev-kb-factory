## What it is

Customer Specific Pricing lets merchants set up bespoke prices for individual customers, for example as a loyalty discount for frequent customers, start-ups, or longstanding clients.

## Key steps / config

- Merchants with customers who have multiple individual price conditions pull those defined prices from their system of choice into Shopware via API.
- Individual prices are then displayed at several steps within the customer journey once the customer is logged in.

## Essential identifiers

- `Customer Specific Pricing`
