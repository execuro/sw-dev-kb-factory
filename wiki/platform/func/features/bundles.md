## What it is

A bundle is a product made up of several individual products. Customers buy it as a complete package, often with a price advantage, and see all included products on the product detail page. This feature page is a short stub that only points to the bundles service page.

## When to use

Use this page to confirm the concept of a bundle; configuration, discounts and storefront behavior are documented on the separate Shopware Services Bundles page.
