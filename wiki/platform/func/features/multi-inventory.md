## What it is

The Multi-Inventory functionality manages the stocks and availability of products across more than one warehouse, ensuring products can be purchased independent of the inventory location as long as they are available in one of them.

## Key steps / config

- Manage stocks and availability per warehouse using the Warehouses functions.
- Availability can be managed via warehouse groups and priorities.
- Availability can additionally be configured using the Rule Builder.

## Essential identifiers

- `Warehouses`
- `Rule Builder`
- Multi-Inventory
