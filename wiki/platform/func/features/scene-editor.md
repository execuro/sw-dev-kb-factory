## What it is

The Scene Editor is a commercial feature for creating 3D scenes, placing products in them and generating images. The source describes rendering an unlimited number of images of the scenes, matched to the products.

## When to use

Use it when product images should be generated from 3D scenes instead of photographed.

## Gotchas

- The feature is in **beta status**. Functionality is limited, and behavior and scope may still change in future updates.
