## What it is

The Scene Editor is a Shopware commercial feature for creating 3D scenes, placing products in them and generating images. An unlimited number of images of a scene can be rendered, matched to the products. Details are on the Scene Editor page in the commercial features section of the merchant documentation.

## When to use

When you need product images rendered from 3D scenes instead of, or in addition to, photographed product images.

## Gotchas

- The feature is in beta status and is only available through Insider Previews.
- Functionality is limited in this version; behavior and scope may still change and may be expanded in future updates.
