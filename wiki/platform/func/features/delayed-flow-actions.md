## What it is

Delayed flow actions let a Flow Builder flow trigger an action only after a certain period of time has passed, rather than immediately.

## Key steps / config

- Release flows from Flow Builder on a later date.
- Example: send an automated email a week after a customer placed an order, asking for a review or suggesting other products.

## Essential identifiers

- `Flow Builder`
- delayed actions
