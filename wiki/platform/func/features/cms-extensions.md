## What it is

CMS extensions add more flexibility to content building in Shopware: creating custom forms and viewing products directly in the category listing through a quickview, for a more tailored content and customer experience.

## Key steps / config

- Create your own forms as part of the CMS content.
- Enable a quickview so products can be viewed directly in the category listing without navigating to the product detail page.

## Essential identifiers

- `CMS extensions`
- quickview
