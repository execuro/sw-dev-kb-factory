## What it is

Digital Sales Rooms is a Shopware feature for personal, live consultation of customers. It lets a merchant run interactive live video events for customers directly from the Shopware website, without switching between a separate presentation tool, a video conferencing system and the store system. It integrates with the existing Shopware system landscape and ecommerce infrastructure. This page is a short feature overview; setup and usage are documented on the Digital Sales Rooms extension page of the merchant documentation.

## When to use

- You want to offer customers personal, up-close consultation online.
- You want to interact with customers live, independent of their location.
- You want to present and highlight products in a live event and engage customers during it, in one tool together with the shop.
