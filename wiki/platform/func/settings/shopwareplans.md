## What it is

Shopware Plans is a Community Edition service for booking a Shopware Rise plan directly from the admin, found under **Settings > System > Shopware Plans**, or via an "Upgrade now" banner shown in various admin areas (e.g. the dashboard).

## When to use

Use it when a Community Edition store wants to upgrade its functionality directly in-admin (Rise plan), or needs to contact Shopware for the Evolve or Beyond plans.

## Key steps / config

1. Open **Settings > System > Shopware Plans**, or click "Upgrade now", to reach the plan booking overview, which lists a minimalist comparison of plan functionalities.
2. Log in with a Shopware account; if not yet connected, the Shopware account login opens in a new window.
3. Complete account requirements if incomplete: create a company, register the shop (the licence domain in admin settings must match the domain registered in the Shopware account), and add a payment method.
4. Enter the expected gross merchandise volume (GMV) for the coming year; the system shows the most suitable offer based on this.
5. Review the pre-filled billing details, agree to the licence agreement and terms and conditions, then click "Complete upgrade".

## Essential identifiers

- Menu path: **Settings > System > Shopware Plans**
- Plans referenced: Rise, Evolve, Beyond

## Version notes

Only the Rise plan can be booked directly from the plan booking overview; Evolve and Beyond require contacting Shopware directly.
