## What it is

Product units let merchants define custom units of measure for products — for example selling drinks by the bottle or shoes by the pair, rather than as a generic countable item. They are managed under Settings > General > Product units.

## When to use

Use product units whenever a product needs to communicate how it is sold or measured — e.g. per bottle, per pair, per box — so customers see the correct unit consistently throughout the shop.

## Key steps / config

- Manage the available units under Settings > General > Product units.
- Assign a unit to a product on the product's Specifications tab: available units are selectable from a drop-down menu each time you create or edit a product.
- Once assigned, the unit is generally shown in the storefront. In the supplied Responsive Theme, the unit appears both in the product listing and on the product detail page.

## Essential identifiers

- Settings > General > Product units
- Product edit screen > Specifications tab
