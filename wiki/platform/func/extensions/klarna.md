## What it is

Klarna Payments is a payment extension that adds Klarna's payment options to Shopware checkout, so merchants are always paid in full upfront while customers choose when to pay.

## When to use

Use it to offer customers flexible ways to pay: pay later by invoice, spread the cost via instalments, or pay instantly by bank transfer, direct debit or credit card through Klarna.

## Key steps / config

- Obtain and configure the extension either directly in Shopware's initial setup wizard, or license it in the Shopware Store at `store.shopware.com` and install it under **Extensions > My Extensions** (requires being logged in with your Shopware Account).
- Activate the extension with the button to the left of it after installation.
- Klarna offers three payment options:
  - Klarna Invoice — customers receive the goods first and get an extra 14 days to pay, without fees.
  - Klarna instalment purchase — customers spread the cost over monthly payments.
  - Klarna instant bank transfer, direct debit and credit card — direct, fast payment methods.

## Essential identifiers

- Admin path: **Extensions > My Extensions**
- Payment options: Klarna Invoice, Klarna instalment purchase, Klarna instant bank transfer/direct debit/credit card
