## What it is

Describes the storefront wish list ("notepad") feature that lets customers temporarily
store products they are interested in, and how to add, view, and remove items.

## When to use

When explaining to a merchant or customer how the storefront wish list works, or
troubleshooting why an anonymous visitor cannot use it.

## Key steps / config

- Customers who are not logged in must accept the wish-list cookie (listed under the
  comfort-functions section of the storefront cookie manager) before the feature works.
- **Adding a product**: click the heart button on a product's image in the listing view
  (lower right corner), or the heart button next to the add-to-basket button on the
  product detail page.
- **Viewing the wish list**: click the heart icon next to the account menu; the wish
  list detail view shows every product that has been added.
- **Removing a product**: click the **X** button on the product image within the wish
  list.

## Essential identifiers

- Wish list / notepad (storefront feature, heart-icon button)
- Storefront cookie manager (comfort functions, wish-list cookie)
