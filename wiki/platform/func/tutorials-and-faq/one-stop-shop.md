## What it is

EU-OSS (One-Stop-Shop) is an EU-wide electronic portal that lets traders and businesses fulfil their VAT obligations for intra-Community trade through a single, central declaration rather than declaring VAT separately in every country.

## When to use

Applies once your store's cross-border sales into other EU countries exceed the EU-wide threshold and you need to register for and account for VAT under the One-Stop-Shop scheme.

## Key steps / config

- The One-Stop-Shop procedure has applied since 1 July 2021.
- The relevant EU-wide threshold is €10,000; once you exceed it, you should register in the applicable online OSS portal.
- Tax configuration itself is still done as usual, via the tax module in Shopware's settings — OSS registration does not change how taxes are configured in the shop, only where VAT is declared.
- The shop should clearly display which delivery country the shown prices apply to, and should transparently offer customers the option to select their delivery country to see the corresponding prices.

## Essential identifiers

- EU-OSS / One-Stop-Shop scheme
- EU-wide threshold: €10,000
- Tax module (Settings > Taxes)

## Gotchas

Merchants should register in the applicable OSS portal once the €10,000 EU-wide threshold is exceeded, and must ensure the storefront transparently shows the delivery country and matching prices to customers.
