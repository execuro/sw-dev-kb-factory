## What it is
This page documents how to create and manage a support ticket for Shopware 6 (PaaS) through console.shopware.com.

## When to use
Use this when you need technical support for a PaaS project, want to check the status of an existing ticket, or need to close a resolved ticket.

## Key steps / config
1. Log in at console.shopware.com; open the account drop-down (arrow, top right) and click **Support**.
2. The ticket overview lists all previously opened tickets and their status.
3. Click **+ New Ticket** to open the new-ticket form and fill in:
   - **Project** — the PaaS project the ticket applies to.
   - **Select category** — the type of question/problem.
   - **Select priority** — indicates severity; a mouseover info button links to guidance on choosing priority.
   - **Ticket subject** — a meaningful title.
   - **Description** — the request or problem, relative to the selected category.
   - **Attach file** — for pictures or logs.
   - **Environment** — the affected environment.
   - **Affected URL** — the corresponding domain.
4. Click **Submit** to create the ticket, or **Cancel** to discard it.
5. After creation, revisit the ticket overview to view processing status and submit additional information.
6. If the support team's answer resolves the issue, click **Solve Ticket** (upper right corner) to close it yourself.

## Essential identifiers
- Ticket statuses: **Open**, **On-Hold**, **Solved**, **Closed**.
- Form fields: **Project**, **Select category**, **Select priority**, **Ticket subject**, **Description**, **Attach file**, **Environment**, **Affected URL**, **Submit**, **Cancel**.
