## What it is
This is the index page for deploying the Digital Sales Rooms frontend app. It notes that templates generated by Shopware Frontends can be deployed in multiple ways depending on the setup, usually either a static hosting service or a server with a Node.js runtime, and that this section shows some best practices for deploying the *Digital Sales Room* frontend app specifically.

## When to use
Read this page before choosing a hosting target for the DSR frontend app; it links onward to the Shopware Frontends deployment documentation and to Nuxt's own deployment guide for the general approaches, before covering DSR-specific best practices.
