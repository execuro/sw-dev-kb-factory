## What it is

With the SaaS platform, Shopware provides updates, hosting, and infrastructure for the shop. This managed model still leaves room for extending the shop.

## When to use

Use this page to understand extension options on Shopware's SaaS (Cloud) offering, where hosting, updates, and infrastructure are managed by Shopware rather than self-hosted.

## Essential identifiers

- SaaS / Cloud platform
- App system (extension mechanism for SaaS stores)

## Gotchas

Because Shopware manages hosting, updates, and infrastructure on the SaaS platform, extension is done through the App system rather than direct server-level customization.
