## What it is

This is an index page listing Shopware's unique extensions:

- **Migration Assistant** — connects the source shop and the target shop to migrate data.
- **B2B Suite** — equips a store with important B2B functions including workflows, order lists, budgets, and quick orders.
- **B2B Components** — enhances a shop with essential B2B functionalities.
- **Advanced Search** — offers the possibility to customize the search fields.
- **Subscriptions** — allows offering products on a subscription basis.
