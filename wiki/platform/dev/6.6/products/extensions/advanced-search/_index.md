## What it is

Advanced Search is a module of the Shopware Commercial plugin, available from Commercial 5.5.0 onward, included with the Evolve and Beyond plans. It builds on Elasticsearch to provide product search plus the ability to search manufacturers and categories, with configuration done through a simple Administration module.

## When to use

Use this when a shop on the Evolve or Beyond Commercial plan needs a higher-performance, customizable search experience beyond the default Shopware search, including searching manufacturers and categories in addition to products.

## Essential identifiers

- `Advanced Search` (Commercial plugin module, requires Commercial 5.5.0+)
- Built on `Elasticsearch`

## Gotchas

Requires basic knowledge of Elasticsearch and Shopware's implementation of it before proceeding with configuration.
