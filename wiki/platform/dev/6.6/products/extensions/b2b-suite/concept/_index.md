## What it is

Introduces the B2B Suite as a comprehensive software solution facilitating efficient, seamless business-to-business transactions, giving organizations tools and functionalities to enhance communication, collaboration and transaction management with their trading partners.

## When to use

Read this section before working on the B2B Suite's internals, since it is the entry point to the concept documentation the rest of the guides assume familiarity with.
