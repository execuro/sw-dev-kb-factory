## What it is

Introduces the subscription extension, which adds a subscription-based model for products so customers can subscribe and receive a product at regular intervals instead of buying it as a one-off purchase.

## When to use

Use this extension for products that customers consume regularly — the source gives groceries, cosmetics, and magazines as examples — where a recurring payment and delivery model adds flexibility over one-off purchases.

## Key steps / config

The source recommends first getting familiar with the [subscription concepts](platform/dev/6.6/products/extensions/subscriptions/concept.md) page before starting to use the extension, since the subscription model is built around a recurring payment model where customers subscribe and receive the product at regular intervals.

## Gotchas

This is an overview page: it introduces the recurring-payment concept but defers the mechanics of billing intervals and plans to the linked concepts page.
