## What it is

Order approval is a B2B Component, part of B2B Employee Management, letting you define rules that determine which orders require approval and which employees can approve them, and to view, approve or decline pending orders.

## When to use

Use when a merchant needs a controlled buying process where certain employee-placed orders must be reviewed before being finalized.

## Key steps / config

- Requires the Employee Management component to be installed and activated.

## Essential identifiers

- Employee Management (prerequisite component)
