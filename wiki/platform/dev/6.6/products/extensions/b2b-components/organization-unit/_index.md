## What it is

The Organization unit component is part of the B2B Employee Management feature set. Each organizational unit can have its own set of employees and use specific payment and shipping methods, enabling more differentiated access rights for businesses with complex structures.

## When to use

Use this when a B2B customer account needs to split employees into units with distinct payment/shipping options and finer-grained access control than a flat employee list allows.

## Key steps / config

Requires the Employee Management component to be installed and activated before Organization unit can be used.

## Essential identifiers

- Employee Management (dependency component)
