## What it is
This section introduces the commerce-related concepts of Shopware, which at its core is an ecommerce platform. It exists to help readers understand the ideas and structures behind the software's commerce features.

## When to use
Read this section when you want to understand why Shopware's commerce features are structured the way they are, rather than how to implement a specific feature step by step.

## Key steps / config
The Concepts section deliberately does not contain code examples; it focuses on conveying the underlying concepts and ideas behind the software. For guided, step-by-step tutorials instead, the source points to the Guides section, starting with its installation guide.
