## What it is
Shopware 6 has an integrated content management system based on layouts, called *Shopping Experiences*. The tool used to compose and manage these layouts is part of the Admin panel and is referred to as *Page Builder*.

## When to use
Use this section when you need to understand how Shopware structures and manages page content and layouts through its own CMS, rather than through hard-coded templates.

## Essential identifiers
- Shopping Experiences
- Page Builder
- Admin panel
