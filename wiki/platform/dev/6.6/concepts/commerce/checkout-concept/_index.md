## What it is
The checkout holds the series of steps needed to purchase items in a Shopware store. It covers the entire process of turning a cart into an order and initiating all of the processes associated with that, such as payment and shipping.

## When to use
Consult this section when you need to understand how Shopware structures the purchase flow, from cart to completed order, including where payment and shipping fit in.

## Key steps / config
This section is further broken down into three focus areas: carts, orders, and payment.

- Cart
- Order
- Payment
- Shipping
