## What it is
Index page for the checkout Payment guide section. It covers the essential payment-related capabilities available to plugins in Shopware: integrating payment methods, tracking payment status, handling refunds and cancellations, and sending payment notifications.

## When to use
Use this section when a plugin needs to integrate a payment method, monitor payment status, process refunds or cancellations, or notify customers about payments. While these functions are typically available within the Shopware core system, plugins offer flexibility to extend or customize them based on specific business requirements, compliance needs, or integration with preferred payment providers.
