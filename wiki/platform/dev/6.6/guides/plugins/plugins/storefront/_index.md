## What it is

This section introduces the Storefront — the part of Shopware 6 that handles the e-commerce platform's front end, covering the online store's visual presentation and user interface.

## When to use

Consult this section when a plugin needs to customize or enhance the Storefront: adding or modifying templates, layouts, styles, and components rather than backend or Administration functionality.

## Key steps / config

The guides grouped under this section cover customizing and enhancing the Storefront via plugins, including adding custom pages, layouts, dynamic content, filters, media, assets, and styles. Together they let a plugin implement responsive designs, optimize performance, and deliver custom shopping experiences by modifying the front-end presentation layer rather than core business logic.

## Essential identifiers

- Storefront — the front-end presentation layer plugins can extend via templates, layouts, styles, and components
