## What it is

This is the overview page for the SEO section of the plugin guides, covering how to add custom SEO URLs for entities like products, categories, and content pages, and how to customize meta tags (titles, descriptions, keywords).

## When to use

Use this section when a plugin needs custom, search-engine-friendly URLs for its own entities or storefront pages, or needs to control meta tag content for on-page SEO.

## Key steps / config

The section splits into adding custom SEO URLs (static and dynamic) for custom entities and controllers, and customizing per-page meta tags.
