## What it is
Apps can enhance the Rule Builder by adding custom rule conditions, letting developers define their own conditions and actions based on business requirements.

## When to use
Use to build dynamic, personalized customer experiences such as customized promotions, targeted discounts, or advanced product recommendations that the built-in Rule Builder conditions don't cover.

## Key steps / config
Starting with version 6.4.12.0, apps can add custom rule conditions for use in the Rule Builder.

## Essential identifiers
- Rule Builder (App Rule Conditions)

## Version notes
Custom rule conditions from apps require Shopware 6.4.12.0 or later.
