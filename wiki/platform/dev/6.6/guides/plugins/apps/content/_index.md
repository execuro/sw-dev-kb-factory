## What it is

This section covers extending Shopware's content management capabilities from a custom app — creating layouts and controlling the visibility of content based on various conditions, to help deliver a tailored customer experience.

## When to use

Use this when your app needs to add or manage Storefront content, such as custom CMS blocks or layout logic, rather than only backend data.

The following pages guide how to extend Shopware's CMS via a custom app, covering how apps can define layouts and content visibility rules within the content management system.
