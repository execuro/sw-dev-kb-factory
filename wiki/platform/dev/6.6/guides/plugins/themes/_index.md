## What it is
Introduces Shopware theme plugins: a theme plugin lets businesses customize and modify the appearance and design of their online store, including layout, styling and visual elements such as fonts, colors and images, to match a brand identity and desired user experience.

## When to use
Use a theme plugin when the goal is to change how a storefront looks — its layout, styling and visual branding — rather than to add new business logic or features, which instead calls for a regular plugin or app.

## Key steps / config
Using a theme plugin, a business can manage and apply custom themes, switch between them, and keep branding consistent across the store. This page is the section overview; the following theme guides cover the concrete steps of creating, configuring, and styling a theme, and how a theme differs technically from a plugin or app.

## Essential identifiers
- theme plugin — a Shopware plugin type dedicated to storefront appearance and branding
