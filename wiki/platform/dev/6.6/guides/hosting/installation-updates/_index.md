This is the index page for the "Installation & Updates" section under hosting. It briefs readers on three topics covered by the sub-pages: cluster setup for custom stores, the Shopware 6 production template, and deployment to an infrastructure.

## When to use

Use this page as a starting point when planning how to install or update a self-hosted, clustered Shopware 6 store, before consulting the more detailed deployment and template sub-pages it introduces.
