## What it is

The Organization unit component is part of B2B Employee Management (Shopware Commercial). Each unit can have its own set of employees and use specific payment and shipping methods, enabling more differentiated access rights for businesses with complex structures.

## When to use

Modelling departments or sub-units inside a B2B customer account that need their own employees, payment/shipping method availability, or permissions.

## Key steps / config

- Requirement: the Employee Management component must be installed and activated.
