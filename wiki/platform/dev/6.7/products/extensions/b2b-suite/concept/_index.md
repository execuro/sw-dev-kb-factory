## What it is

Index of the B2B Suite concept pages. The B2B Suite provides tools for communication, collaboration and transaction management between a business and its trading partners. The concept section covers four topics: system architecture, basic conventions (naming rules and entity names), method structure (which functions are replaceable and their compatibility guarantees), and the line item list component.

## When to use

Read before extending or customizing the B2B Suite, to find the concept page for architecture, naming conventions, method compatibility rules, or the `LineItemList` data structure.
