## What it is

The entry page of the References section of the B2B Suite Migration documentation. The section collects additional resources and references for understanding the migration from B2B Suite to B2B Commercial: links to related documentation, command references for executing migration tasks, and other relevant material.

## When to use

When you are running or customising the B2B Suite to B2B Commercial migration and need a quick lookup of the migration console commands or other reference material, rather than the step-by-step guides.
