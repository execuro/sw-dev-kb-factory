## What it is

The entry page of the developer section of the B2B Suite to B2B Commercial migration docs. It covers how to customize or extend the migration: adding new components, extending existing entities, and validating configurations.

## When to use

Start here when the standard migration does not cover your data and you need to tailor it. The section's pages give detailed steps for creating a configurator, defining XML mappings, and setting conditions or default values.
