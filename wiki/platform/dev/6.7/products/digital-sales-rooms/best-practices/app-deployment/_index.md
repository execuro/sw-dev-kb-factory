## What it is

Section index for deploying the *Digital Sales Room* frontend app. It points out that templates generated by Shopware Frontends can be deployed in several ways depending on the setup, and introduces the DSR-specific deployment best practices listed in its child pages.

## When to use

When you need to host the DSR frontend app (the Shopware Frontends / Nuxt-based storefront for Digital Sales Rooms), for example as part of a SaaS setup.

## Key steps / config

- Choose a hosting model: most setups use either a static hosting service or a server with a Node.js runtime.
- The general approaches follow the Shopware Frontends deployment document (`/frontends/best-practices/deployment.html`) and the Nuxt deployment instructions (`https://nuxt.com/deploy`).
- For DSR-specific practices, see the child pages of this section.
